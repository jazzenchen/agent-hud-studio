# Third-Party Assets & Attribution / 第三方素材清单

> **Why `full_loft.glb` and `full_loft.blend` are excluded from this repository**

> The merged scene files (`public/assets/full_loft.glb` and `assets/blend/full_loft.blend`) combine 3D models whose licenses forbid redistribution (57 BlenderKit Royalty-Free models and 3 official Apple AR Quick Look `.usdz` models). A fresh clone runs without the merged scene; visit **[studio.agenthud.app](https://studio.agenthud.app)** to explore the complete scene live, or follow the rebuild instructions below to fetch the assets locally.

## 5. Rebuilding the Full Scene Locally

To rebuild `full_loft.blend`, `full_loft.glb`, and the 4 time-of-day lightmaps locally (requires **Blender 4.3+** and Python 3):

```bash
# 1. Fetch Poly Haven CC0 HDRIs, PBR textures, and 3D models (no account required)
python3 pipeline/fetch_polyhaven.py --phase all

# 2. Fetch BlenderKit models (a BlenderKit account is required for Royalty-Free assets if you download manually / via addon;
#    free catalog assets are fetched to assets/cache/blenderkit/ via the public API)
python3 pipeline/fetch_blenderkit.py --phase all

# 3. (Optional) Place Apple AR Quick Look .usdz files under assets/cache/apple_official/
#    If omitted, build_studio_slice.py skips them cleanly without error.
```
